Engineering use and professional responsibility

PileAxis is an engineering analysis and decision-support tool, not a substitute for the independent judgement of a qualified professional. Its results must be reviewed and approved by a licensed engineer before they are used for design, a drawing, a submittal or construction.

Results depend on the information, assumptions, criteria and code provisions selected by the user. The soil is represented by the p-y, t-z and Q-z models and the parameters you enter; real soil may behave differently from these idealized curves, and site-specific geotechnical recommendations take precedence over the program's models and defaults. The program analyses a single pile in one bending plane under static loading: pile groups are approximated only through the p-multipliers you enter, cyclic effects only through the API sand model's cyclic factor, and pile group interaction, dynamic and time-history response, liquefaction and lateral spreading, and pile driveability are outside its scope.
